Arena style battle with a crowd watching the battle
Single-player  card and tower strategy game against the computer
Eight-card deck with a four-card rotating: Knight, Archers, Giant, Fireball, skeleton, Cannon, Mage, Freeze.
Select a card and click the friendly half to deploy. Spells can target anywhere. Keys 1–4 select cards; pointer input supports touch.
Energy starts at 5, caps at 10, and regenerates equally for both players. Cards cost energy.
Each side has two side towers and one crown tower. Troops cross the river at two bridges and fight enemies. Towers defend automatically.
Destroy the opposing crown tower to win immediately. Otherwise, after three minutes, the side with more destroyed enemy towers wins; equal crowns are a draw.
Show time, energy, crowns, next card, and win/loss feedback. Allow restarting.
Original local arcade prototype; no online multiplayer, purchases, or accounts.
